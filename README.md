# INTELLICORE
## Company Overview & Website Development Brief

### 1. About the Company

Intellicore is a technology and software development company that works with other businesses to deliver reliable, scalable, and customized digital solutions.

Our business model is service-based and project-driven. We collaborate with companies, startups, entrepreneurs, and organizations that need technical expertise to build, improve, or maintain their digital products.

We undertake projects from clients and manage the development process through planning, design, development, testing, deployment, and post-launch support.

### 2. What Does Intellicore Do?

Intellicore provides technology services to businesses that want to establish, improve, or scale their digital presence.

Our intended service categories include:

- **Website Development:** Business websites, corporate websites, landing pages, and other web solutions.
- **Web Application Development:** Custom web-based platforms and business applications.
- **Software Development:** Software solutions tailored to client requirements.
- **Mobile Application Development:** Mobile apps based on project scope and technical requirements.
- **Backend & API Development:** Server-side systems, database integration, and APIs.
- **Website & Software Maintenance:** Bug fixes, technical support, updates, and improvements according to the agreed service scope.

Final service offerings should reflect the company's actual capabilities and approved business commitments.

### 3. Our Business Model

Intellicore works on a B2B (Business-to-Business) service model.

Our typical workflow is:

1. A company or client approaches Intellicore with a project requirement.
2. We understand the requirements, scope, budget, and expected timeline.
3. We propose a suitable technical solution and development plan.
4. Our development team designs, builds, and tests the solution.
5. We deliver and deploy the project according to the agreement.
6. We provide up to six months of maintenance/support when included in the relevant agreement.

The exact duration, scope, exclusions, and response times of maintenance depend on the agreement with the client.

### 4. Purpose of the Intellicore Website

The website will serve as the official digital presence of Intellicore and help potential clients understand our capabilities and contact us for new projects.

The website should:

- Present Intellicore as a professional technology service provider.
- Explain our services in clear, business-friendly language.
- Showcase genuine projects, technical capabilities, and case studies.
- Explain our development process and maintenance services.
- Allow businesses to submit project inquiries and request quotations.
- Publish company updates, articles, and career opportunities.
- Provide an admin panel to manage website content and inquiries.
- Provide a secure client portal for authorized clients to track their assigned projects, milestones, and updates, if this feature is included in the approved scope.

### 5. Target Audience

Our primary target audience includes:

- Startups that need websites, applications, or software products.
- Small and medium-sized businesses looking for technical solutions.
- Established companies seeking external development teams.
- Businesses that need ongoing website or software maintenance.
- Agencies and organizations that outsource development work to technology partners.

### 6. Our Development Approach

Intellicore aims to follow a structured development process:

**Understand → Plan → Design → Develop → Test → Deploy → Maintain**

Every project should have a defined scope, clear deliverables, agreed timelines, appropriate quality checks, and a documented handover process.

Our team should prioritize maintainable code, responsive interfaces, secure implementations, clear communication, and reliable delivery.

### 7. Website Pages and Features

The proposed website scope includes:

- Home
- About Us
- Services
- Portfolio / Projects
- Blog / Insights
- Careers
- Contact Us
- Get a Quote
- Maintenance & Support
- Admin Dashboard
- Client Login / Project Tracking
- Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions

Admin and client portal functionality must be protected by appropriate authentication and authorization.

### 8. Brand Positioning and Communication

The website should communicate these qualities:

- Professionalism
- Technical competence
- Reliability
- Transparency
- Quality-focused development
- Long-term client support

The design should be modern, clean, responsive, accessible, and suitable for a B2B technology company.

Use clear calls to action such as:

- Discuss Your Project
- Request a Quote
- Explore Our Services
- View Our Work
- Contact Our Team

Avoid unsupported claims, invented client testimonials, fake project statistics, or guarantees that the company has not approved.

### 9. Expected Outcome

The final website should help a prospective client answer five questions:

1. What does Intellicore do?
2. Which problems can Intellicore solve?
3. Why should a business consider working with Intellicore?
4. How can a client discuss a new project or request a quotation?
5. What support can the client expect after project delivery?

Every developer should build their assigned feature with these objectives in mind.

### 10. Instructions for the Development Team

Before implementation, review this brief and clarify any missing business requirements with the team lead.

Follow the approved UI design, API contracts, coding standards, and GitHub issue acceptance criteria.

Every task must have a clear owner, a feature branch, appropriate testing, and a reviewed pull request.

Do not invent company information or make unapproved promises about services, pricing, timelines, or maintenance commitments.

The goal is to build a professional, maintainable website that accurately represents Intellicore and supports future business growth.
